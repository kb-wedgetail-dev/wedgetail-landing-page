Turning AI plans into working software.

We help organisations turn an AI roadmap into software their teams can use. We build the application, connect existing systems, test the results and support it after launch.

Grounded assistants

Assistants that find answers in your documents, cite their sources and respect access permissions. Hosting is planned around your existing cloud environment.

Agents and workflow automation

Document processing, triage, approvals and routine tasks, with human review where it matters and a record of actions taken.

Integration and evaluation

Connections to the systems you already run, with access controls enforced in the application and data layers. We measure answer quality before launch and monitor it afterwards.

Secure by design. We plan access, hosting and data handling from the start, and document the deployment for your security team.

Find answers in your own information.

We build assistants that answer from your policies, records and documents, with sources people can check. We plan cloud hosting, data location and permissions around your organisation's requirements.

Data and access

We review your information and permissions so the assistant is designed to respect who can access each source.

Your existing platforms

We work with Azure OpenAI, Copilot Studio and Amazon Bedrock, connecting to SharePoint, records systems and business data. Private hosting is also available.

Measured, not assumed

We test real questions with known answers, checking source accuracy, access permissions and when the assistant should decline to answer. We share the results and keep measuring after launch.
